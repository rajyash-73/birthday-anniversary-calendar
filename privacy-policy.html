Privacy Policy

Birthday Anniversary Calendar (Android app, package com.rajya.birthdaycalendar)
Effective date: October 13, 2026

In short: everything you add to Birthday Anniversary Calendar stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

The names, dates, optional phone numbers and notes you enter, your reminder choices and your settings. This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Contacts (optional): only when you choose “Import from contacts”, the app reads names, birthdays, anniversaries and phone numbers from your contacts so you can pick who to add. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Calendar (optional): only when you choose to import from a calendar, copy your list into a calendar, or turn on calendar sync. The app reads birthday events, and writes birthday events to the calendar you pick. If that calendar is synced by your account provider (for example Google Calendar), the provider handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications, alarms and starting after a restart: used only to show your birthday reminders on time.
  • Files: when you import or save a file, you choose the file through Android's file picker; the app only reads or writes that file.

What we collect

We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; the app only receives confirmation that the unlock was bought. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and exporting

If you share or export your list (for example as a calendar or spreadsheet file), it goes only where you choose to send it.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is managed by Google under your account settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any person inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Events you copied into a calendar stay there until you remove them, or you can choose “Stop and remove them” when turning off calendar sync.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
